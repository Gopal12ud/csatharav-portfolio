Contract/Drafting

Contract drafting is the structured process of writing legal agreements that comply with applicable law and use appropriate legal language to capture the terms between parties. A well-drafted contract clearly defines each party's rights, responsibilities, and remedies — covering scope of work, payment terms, timelines, confidentiality, dispute resolution, and termination conditions. The goal isn't just to create a contract, but an enforceable one that safeguards business interests both now and in the future.

MCA & ROC Compliance

The ROC, under MCA, is the primary custodian of company records in India and ensures that companies follow the Companies Act, 2013 in terms of governance, transparency, and timely filings. Every company registered under the Act—whether it is actively doing business or not—must file specified forms and maintain records with ROC via the MCA portal.
